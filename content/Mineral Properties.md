Steel, iron, lead, tungsten, brass, zinc - unenchantable
Gold - basic enchantable metal, often coated onto weapons to make them enchantable with medium strength
Silver - most common metal to coat onto weapons and enchant with, as it is a common weakness of usually attack resistant monsters, not quite as strong as gold but a good starting point for simple single enchantments. not commonly seen as an alloy due to its special use case when pure
Copper - mostly used as filler with more expensive materials for magic conductivity, as such it doesn't have a large capacity for enchantment. on its own it can only hold weak magic in its channel, often not enough for practical use

Cobalt - 
Tin - fickle to enchant as it wastes magic energy by channeling it outwards too fast, best used as a base in non-magic weapons for its light weight or for warding against enchantments
Bronze - alloy: 87.5% copper, 12.5% tin - metal of choice for constructs, as it repels most magic but can hold weak enchantments 
Titanium - 
Darksteel - 
Mithral - good for charge weapons, as it is strong enough to hold onto enchantments but has the ability to shoot off magic under specific circumstances
Apolymite - enchantable only by vapor deposition, but one of the strongest conductors and can hold many enchantments
Palladium - aka "white gold" - best used for purifying enchantment materials, increasing elemental clarity and enchantment strength. when alloyed with gold (up to 50%) it can enhance the channel strength but not increase capacity
Rose Gold - alloy: 75% copper, 25% gold - secondmost common enchantment metal available, due to its relative cheapness. limited strength and capacity, usually only 1-3 enchantments, but more than enough for the typical low-to-mid level adventurer
Electrum- alloy: 75% gold, 25% silver - aka "green gold" -
Adamant: alloy: 50% diamond (powder), 50% palladium - unenchantable by virtue it has a unbreakable magic bond to itself, extremely strong material and used in extremely sharp-edged swords
Adamantine - alloy: 70% adamant, 30% mithral - the strongest magic conductor available aside from apolymite
