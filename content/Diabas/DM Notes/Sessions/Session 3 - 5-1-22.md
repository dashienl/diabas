Taigam leads the cart and horse over to [[Rolling Barrel Carriage Rentals]] and checks it in. He's given a slip which tells him a return date to pick up the cart, otherwise they will send it back.

The party re-gathers at the entrance to the Festival area. They try to decide where to go next when they remember that they also came to try and save somebody. Taigam sees [[Castle Aon]] and starts heading towards it, to which Parogi frantically tries to get him to not go there. Parogi tries to fight Taigam to stop him, but he dodges and deflects the hits and keeps walking.

Rhodie and Parogi soon after try following Taigam, while Bucket opts to not deal with this and instead try to seek information at the Bookstore.

As they approach the castle, they notice a very tall wall and gate as well as many guards patrolling a large area between this and the castle itself. Parogi is still trying to get Taigam to back off from the castle, warning that Apollo is inside and that this place is very hard to get into. Taigam, in his stubborn tenacity, continues to ignore and insult Parogi. Rhodie shouts from a distance and says they need to make a plan, and says that maybe their [[Dragons|Faerie Dragon]] could help. 

As Parogi looks beyond the gates, she makes eye contact with a guard and gets overrun with fear. She tells the party they need to leave right now. Rhodie, now worried, casts Calm Emotions to free Parogi and give the party confidence, believing that they are now immune to the fear effect that these guards cause. The party still hasn't figured out how to get over the gate.

Meanwhile, Bucket is at the Bookstore. He requests a book on the castle, then sits down and starts studying it. The store owner gives him a cup of tea while he reads. 

Rhodie messages Bucket and says that they're storming the castle right now. Bucket ignores this message on purpose. Rhodie starts shaking the gate, drawing attention from the guards. Parogi gives one last shot at snapping Taigam, but Taigam deflects the starbolt and the party finds out that there's some sort of anti-magic field above the gate. Parogi takes this opportunity to start taking Rhodie down the hill and demanding they leave once more, leaving Taigam alone. Eventually he follows.

The rest of the party enters the Bookstore and sees Bucket relaxing with his tea and books. He announces that while the rest of the party was messing around, he's been learning about the castle. Parogi asks to see the book, which Bucket says no and fights to purposefully block her from seeing it. The party asks questions about what he read and he begrudgingly shares. Rhodie asks if he can detect magic and asks if he can check the castle's anti-magic field.

They go back up the hill and Bucket detects the castle, finding out that there is indeed a thick bubble of anti-magic over the castle, but in the yard there is no such thing. He also finds out that the guards are all affected by necromancy. Feeling irked by the dread in the air, Bucket asks if they can leave now that he did what he was asked to do. 

Taigam, still determined to get in the castle, makes a jump for over the gates and touches through the bubble, immediately freezing in the air and feeling total fear. He begins to plummet out of the air and is barely caught by Parogi. 

The entire party is washed over by the realization that the castle is too well guarded, and that if the person they wanted to save was here, that he surely is dead. They then also notice the happier atmosphere down the hill where everyone is celebrating, and feel strange about how they're spending their time doing this instead of celebrating. Parogi finally says fuck it, and walks off to the candy store, telling the others to join her if they want some chocolate.

The party heads into [[Nathaniel's Sugar Shack & Magic Appraisal]] to buy candy and generally have fun. Taigam opts to stay just outside for a bit. Eventually he has his own realization that he doesn't know how to have fun anymore, and resolves to get himself something small and try to find joy.

Nathaniel at the counter asks the party if there's anything else they need, like a magic appraisal. Parogi and Bucket look between eachother and decide to ask about the crystal, carefully pulling it from a bag. Nathaniel is worried, and begins to lock down the candy store with the party inside, dims the lights, then probes the party about it. He asks Rhodie a few specific questions after finding out she was the one who used it.

After questioning he leads the party to the basement, which was hidden behind candy shelves. In the basement he tells the party why the crystals exist and that the party was chosen by the Gods to resurrect [[Eibher (Light)]]. For various reasons, much of the party is upset by this realization.
