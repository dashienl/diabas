The party gets back on track of figuring out where [[Fort Nytevic]] is so they can deliver their letter to [[Qantenik]]. [[Bucket.pdf|Bucket]] and [[Taigam.pdf|Taigam]] are the first to spot a cave in the forest. Bucket heads towards it and in response Taigam divebombs him, giving [[Rhodie.pdf|Rhodie]] an adrenaline rush (of excitement) in the process. Bucket henceforth forbids Taigam from touching Rhodie ever.

The group bickers over where their focus should be. [[Parogi.pdf|Parogi]] and Taigam both say that they should avoid the cave and continue with delivering the letter. Bucket and Rhodie want to go in for various different reasons.

The party notices smoke billowing out of the cave, followed by 3 panicked [[Kobolds]] rushing out and avoiding the party. Rhodie marches in, citing concerns that something may be hurting the kobolds. Bucket follows in after her, and then the other two follow as well so they don't split the party.

A group of kobolds wearing crude chefs outfits and holding various kitchen supplies rushes in front of the party and skitters down the cave towards where the smoke is coming from. Taigam, not paying attention, knocks over one holding a huge pot of water. He offers to get a new pot of water and boil it for him, the kobold declines saying that he would rather do it himself.

Rhodie and Bucket constantly argue over whether Bucket should stay out of the cave, as he has permanent pneumonia and the smoke may hurt him. Taigam gets upset and tells Rhodie that if she were a real adventurer, then she wouldn't try to squander another adventurers dreams. This does nothing to stop the argument.

The three kobolds that left the cave earlier come rushing back past the party holding mushrooms and berries. Parogi and Tagaim go ahead of the party to see what's going on.

A lone kobold stands guard at a door placed in the cave. He refuses entry for the 2 party members present. Eventually the rest of the party catches up. Taigam acts as a translater for the party since he speaks Draconic. They point out the smoke, which causes the kobold at the door to rush in and abandon post.

The party looks between the cracks in the door to see what's behind it. They decide as a group to go in and see a massive winding stone ramp. At the very bottom is a pit of busy kobolds cooking a large meal - this is the source of the smoke. On a ledge above them is a [[Dragons|Faerie Dragon]] breathing euphoria breath over the kobolds in a thick cloud. Bucket identifies the cloud as being able to mind control, and Rhodie promptly cites concerns that the kobolds may be enslaved and need freed. Taigam offers to ask the dragon directly, so he hails it over.

The party talks to the dragon through Taigam and learns that he isn't enslaving the kobolds, but rather leading them at their request since kobolds are more fulfilled when serving a dragon. They're making a version of [[Lucky Dumplings]] because he has been banned from getting real dumplings from [[Aon]]. 

The party offers to get the dragon some dumplings if he tags along with them. Bucket adds that he wants some other form of payment too, and wants to try their dumplings. The dragon obliges and gives the party 4 dumplings and a sack of various coins and trinkets. Rhodie refuses to eat the dumplings, Parogi and Taigam each eat 1, and Bucket takes the spare. They all taste mediocre or horrible.

The dragon says his goodbyes to the kobolds and the party exits the cave, then makes their way out of the forest. They spot Fort Nytevic on a distant hill and hurry over.

The party is stopped at a gate by guards requesting ID. Parogi uses her dad's old [[Aon Government IDs|Aon ID]] to get past the guards. 

The party heads over to a building in the back, labeled as a research facility. The clerk at the counter calls [[Daciana]] down to collect her letter, which she reads and then promptly storms out of the building. She uses a stone [[Teleportation Circle]] to leave.

Rhodie is fascinated by the stone circle and bothers the operator for several minutes. 

The rest of the party wants to go further into the research facility, so Parogi uses her knowledge of Aon's government to get them in. Bucket plots to steal a crystal he sees in a display case. Eventually Rhodie comes back inside the building. 

Everyone, for their own reasons, decides to help Bucket steal the crystal. Taigam offers a distraction by eating a ration and feigning choking, stumbling around the facility and vomiting lightning out of the window. Rhodie shouts and offers more distraction, pretending to help Taigam. Bucket smashes open the display case and grabs the crystal and some books. In the flurry, Parogi decides to grab some classified documents. Once someone notices the missing crystal, the party piles together and throws themselves out of the window one by one and rushes for the teleportation circle.

Taigam intimidates the operator into sending them off before the guards can catch them, requesting Aon. The party frantically argues about whether or not they should go to the capital of the government they just betrayed, given they're probably wanted criminals. In a sleep deprived panic, the operator sends them to the [[Weary One's Repose]]. Just as they black out for teleportation, they see the guards grab the operator by the shirt collar at weapon point.

Once concious again, the party begins to move deeper into the forest surrounding the lodge, with Rhodie leading way. They set up camp once they find another small clearing.

Parogi points out that the party just crossed a superpower government. Bucket looks over his spoils. Rhodie asks to hold the crystal, to which Bucket obliges. Rhodie accidentally triggers the crystal while playing with it and gets a vision of [[Eibher (Light)|Eibher]], then blacks out.

Bucket is extremely worried and upset about what happened to Rhodie just now and tries waking her up. Parogi takes the now empty crystal and holds onto it.

Eventually Rhodie wakes up, sharing parts of her vision.

Session end.
