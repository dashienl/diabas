
Apolymite is a rare crystal found deep within pressurized caves. Most commonly it seems to be found specifically in cave systems that are underneath dense cities, suggesting that either people are attracted to the presence of the crystal or that the crystals form easier under the presence of people.

It's resistant to scratching, with a hardness of 10. It is however incredibly fragile, as any bending or substantial pressure will easily cause fractures. Its also very lightweight. The material has an extremely high melting point, unable to be melted by all currently existing forges. It is magically conductive, needing only a thin coating of materials to be attached via vapor deposition in a vacuum. It cannot conduct via embedding, due to the fragility.

The crystals form in naturally long, flat blades similar to a sword. The edges are extremely sharp, easily scratching most other materials and slicing through things denser and tougher if handled carefully. 

Some adventurers use the crystal as their primary sword, due to its capabilities to cleave through flesh and sometimes even armor. Such swords are carefully cut out from their crystal clusters, leaving the stone base attached to be carved into and used as a handle. Such work is considered artisinal, and therefore it's extremely expensive to obtain a sword like this.

Ongoing theory by the [[Worship of the Old Gods]] states that [[Eibher (Light)]] may have been killed using a sword made of this, saying it's the only thing that could pierce through true godly flesh and withstand her body heat.
