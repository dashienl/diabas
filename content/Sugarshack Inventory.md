Lolly Lantern 
2 for 1gp 
_Popular during festival seasons. Children hold them up like sparklers and draw in the air._
Glows gently after licked. No other effect. 

Shade Chocolate 
15 gp 
_99% cacao, 1% black magic. Pure, uncut darkness._ 
+2 on Stealth checks for the turn immediately following. 

Upple Gum 
_Tastes like sour apples and fluffy clouds. Filled with a sweet powder._ 
5gp 
Lasts for 1 hour. You can hover 5 ft off the ground at will. While hovering your movement speed is 10ft. Can negate fall damage. 

24-hour Energy 
20gp 
_Makes you see faster. Unfortunately, it comes with a VERY heavy crash._ 
Lasts 1 day. You can choose to take 2 actions on your turn and double your movement speed, at the cost that you cannot move or act on your next turn. After your 3rd use of this effect in a day, you fall unconcious instantly. Taking more than one in a day does not refill uses and may have adverse effects. 


Glowstick Juice (Soda, Grape, Melon, Lychee) 
5gp 
_Massive refreshment packed in a small vial. Great to drink before an interpretive dance._ 
Act as if you had Faerie Fire casted on you. The colour is related to it's flavor. Blue for soda, purple for grape, green for melon, and pink for lychee.) 

Liquid Otter Pops 
2gp 
_A must-have for swimming in frigid waters._ 
Resistance to cold damage for 1 minute. 

Power-up! Energy Bar (Chocolate, Peanut Butter Banana, Vanilla Cake) 
10gp 
_Tastes a little bitter, like protein powder. Make sure to read the label carefully before use._ 
Chocolate: Your size category changes to Tiny. You keep your strength. 
PB&B: Grow one size category. Does not increase strength. 
Vanilla Cake: Your arms can stretch, adding +5ft to range on attacks. 

Each effect lasts 10 minutes and cannot be dispelled by Dispel Magic before the duration ends. Energy bars do not stack with each other. Eating two of the same does not extend time, and eating two different ones may have adverse effects. 

Hugs & Kisses

Once melted in your mouth, it gives you the feeling of a warm hug. +1 bonus on CHA saves for the turn following if you eat 5. 

Popping Rockets 
4 for 1gp 
_This unmistakable fizz looks just like shooting stars._ 
Glitters in your mouth. No other benefits.

Dracodrops (Cinnamon, Wintergreen, Licorice, Violet, Butterscotch) 
5gp 
_A hard candy that you'd find mixed into Great Grandpappy Drago's hoard._ 
Allows you to make a 5x10ft line breath attack as a bonus action, damage type corresponding with the flavor. Fire for cinnamon, cold for mint, acid for licorice, poison for violet, and lightning for butterscotch. Deals 1d6+2 damage. 
You must make the breath attack by your next turn or suffer this damage yourself, the breath attack spouting out of your nostrils and mouth.

Tincture of Spice 
100 G 
*The level of spice gets stronger with the more audacity it's consumer has.* 
As an action, you can drink the tincture to lower your maximum HP by 20 and increase your critical hits to land on one more number (such as on 19 or 20 for one level). You can repeat this process as many times as desired. Effect wears off after 1 hour. Taking more maximum HP than your character has knocks them unconscious for 1 week.
