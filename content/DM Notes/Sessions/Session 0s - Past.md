Characters meet at a tavern, [[The Trou-bard-our]] in [[Landeli]]. [[Parogi.pdf|Parogi]] and [[Bucket.pdf|Bucket]] are at the bar, [[Rhodie.pdf|Rhodie]] performs on stage, and [[Taigam.pdf|Taigam]] sits at a table. After a mishap that insights a barfight, everyone is kicked out.

Rhodie sits down at a table outside to decompress. Bucket leans against the building. Everyone introduces eachother.

Taigam asks Parogi to spar with him as they are both monks. They go back and forth until Parogi needs to take a break. Bucket decides to spring in and fails to hit Taigam, which upsets Taigam into launching a lightning breath attack at Bucket. Parogi jumps in the way of the attack and is knocked unconcious.

The entire party (the only 2 conscious and/or paying attention) proceeds to panic.

The village elder ([[Daciana]]) comes up to the party and revives Parogi, then offers the party a place to stay for the night if they would be willing to help her with a task in the morning. They accept and make their way to bed. (Long Rest)

When everyone wakes up, Daciana greets them properly and offers the task. The party is to deliver a letter to her cousin [[Qantenik]] in the settlement of [[Fort Nytevic]]. The fastest route has them pass through a small forest which may have [[Dragons]] inside. Aware of the peril, they accept. Bucket was convinced by being offered a blue flower crown like the one Daciana was wearing. Rhodie enthusiastically takes the letter and attempts to lead the way.

Taigam picks up Rhodie and takes her soaring over the top of the forest. Taigam wants a bird's eye view and a safe distance from the possible dragon. Rhodie, easily entertained, has completely lost focus of where they're going.

In the forest, Bucket and Parogi get distracted by finding a bush of [[Silverberries]]. They eat some, and then attack eachother with the remaining ones they picked. Bucket threw the first one.

Session end.
