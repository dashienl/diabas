The party, gathered around a campfire, tries to figure out what to do next after the events of last night. 

[[rhodie.pdf|Rhodie]] is worried that the man who teleported them is in danger and argues they must go back to save him. [[Parogi.pdf|Parogi]] deems this a bad idea, as they would likely be promptly caught and executed. [[Taigam.pdf|Taigaim]] interjects that if the man is alive, they might've taken him to a secure location to interogate, like the capital [[Aon]].

The party opts to take the camp down and head back towards the [[Weary One's Repose]] to figure out where they are. Outside the building is a corkboard with many faded and expired quest postings, and to the sides are rows of carriages and horses.

They enter the building. Rhodie is in awe of how big it is compared to other reststops she's visited. The building's owner greets them from a desk by the entrance and asks if they need any help. Taigam says the group is headed for Aon and they needed to know how to get there and asks if the carriages outside are for sale or rent. The man says they are and shares the rate to go to Aon, and says they can just drop the horses and carriage off at [[Rolling Barrel Carriage Rentals]] rather than returning.

Taigam puts all the gold down to pay for this carriage (12G) and tells the party to just pay him back later. Rhodie gives her share of 3G to him promptly. The man says that Aon will take a few hours even with a speedy horse, and offers the party to take some food from his breakfast buffet for the inn stays. Taigam declines and goes to pick out the horses and carriages.

Rhodie, [[Bucket.pdf|Bucket]], and Parogi head to the buffet and take copious amounts of food. Bucket stuffs himself as full as possible, Rhodie dumps her canteen and fills it with maple syrup and takes a few muffins for Taigam, and Parogi steals a bunch of muffins and waffles as well as filling her canteen with orange juice.

Once done with their shenanigans, they meet Taigam outside where he has picked 2 stunning black horses and a 4 seated carriage. Rhodie gives Taigam the muffins she picked out, and Parogi shows him the bag full of muffins as well. They board the carriage and head off towards their destination.

Bucket studies his book of folktales during the ride.

Eventually arriving, the party finds themselves backed up by traffic due to the [[Festival of Fading Light]] taking place in Aon. Large amounts of people have set up tents and campfires along the road who are also waiting to get in. Ahead of them, a few guards work their way down to give placeholding tickets.

One guard approaches the carriage asking what they're in Aon for. Taigam acts as the party face and says they're here for the festival, to which Bucket asks what he's talking about. Taigam then shuts the door and improvises a plan to give them priority. He says that the party is here to perform with the dragon on top of their carriage, and that Bucket was invited along as the +1 since he's never been to Aon before. The guard believes this and asks for the documents to prove they have a booth. Taigam asks for a moment to retrieve them and gets back into the carriage.

Taigam catches the party up on the plan he just created, and asks Parogi if they could possibly use her [[Aon Government IDs|ID]] in lieu of the required papers. She's exasperated but agrees, as they need to get in. Taigam and Parogi get out again and carefully flash the ID to the guard, who then guides them to a different entrance of the city that will give them easier access and tells them how to get to the festival.

-

As the party begins to head over to the festival, they realize they can't bring the carriage in due to the fact they don't actually have a lot. Everyone gets out, and Taigam opts to walk the cart over to The Rolling Barrel to drop it off.

Parogi also splits off, heading for the lower class neighborhood to gamble at a shady facility.

Bucket and Rhodie wander the festival in search of the [[Lucky Dumplings]] cart with the [[Dragons|Faerie Dragon]]. When they find it, Rhodie strolls up confidently with the dragon and as a result, the cart closes their windows and denies service. Rhodie fervently argues in favor of getting dumplings, to which Bucket points out that the dragon had been banned. Rhodie walks back to the festival entrance to watch the dragon instead.

Bucket knocks on the window and asks if he can get some orders of dumplings. The cart opens up and obliges, charging 5SP for a tray of 6, or they're free if you can guess the fillings inside. Bucket decides to play a few rounds and successfully gets 4 of the 6 trays he buys for free.

Meanwhile, Parogi finds a hole in the wall gambling facility. She steps inside and notices some people staring at her. She heads for the blackjack table and it dawns on her that the people who stared at her are former members of the [[Brotherhood of the Golden Fang]], her mercenary guild.

She proceeds to curbstomp everyone at the table and is accused of cheating by someone who was once her superior, being told to get out and never come back. She bargains for one more game at a different table, which she wins too despite finding that the dealer is cheating and then splits her winnings with the others at the table. 

Session end.
