# Pre-History
Evidence of society prior to The Shaping is limited in nature, existing largely as fossil record and found artifacts. It is known that before The Shaping, two major [[10. Sapient, Sentient, and Sophont Species/\|sophont species]] lived across the singular landmass. The [[10. Sapient, Sentient, and Sophont Species/Dragons]] largely dominated areas such as natural treetops, mountains, caves, and temperate pastures, while the [[Humans]] excelled at extreme environments and built shelter to fit their needs. Both species hunted and foraged the existing [[11. Flora & Fauna/\|flora and fauna]], and there is evidence that where their needs were shared, the two species worked together symbiotically, sharing the spoils of hunts, providing one another with shelter and protection, and creating tools for one another.
# 1 Gewiderea, Era 0: The Shaping
The first documented event of modern history was The Shaping, which marks the epoch used in denoting time elapsed since this event. Beginning on 1 Gewiderea, eight then-unidentified energetic masses impacted Diabas in eight locations of the planet, causing a mass near-extinction event. Each impact had varying depth, with some opening volcanic pits which formed the distinct diabase deposits which give Diabas its modern name. Today, it is known that these energetic masses make up the [[Core]]. While very early documented interactions with the Core show peaceful relationships among them and the native Humans and Dragons, it is unknown whether this record is biased by later record purges resulting in lost history. 

The Core divided Diabas into eight equivalent sections, henceforth known as octants, which served as the basis for later countries formed. Each Core reshaped the land, flora and fauna, humans, and dragons to suit their preferences, giving form to the modern day species and geography. The core left the area underground largely untouched, giving rise to the [[Old Depths]] societies in the underplane. Modern humans are [living fossils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_fossil), due to their entirely untouched lineage compared to other human-derived sophont species.

The Core also gave way to modern magic, with the eight impact positions of their bodies designating the hub points of the [[Leylines]]. Each Core is representative of one of the eight Elemental schools practiced in modern studies, designated into four pairs: Light and War, Life and Death, Nature and Tempest, Knowledge and Trickery. 
## Eras 0-740: Arrears of the Core
Throughout the following years, the Core would position themselves as divine leaders of their octants, allowing little say in the proceedings of the governments from the common sophont species. Strongly defined cultures in each octant led to the alienation of any shaped species who traveled outside of their formation country, as well as extreme isolation of unchanged native Humans and Dragons. 

Tensions would gradually increase during these eras, culminating in several events of political unrest.
