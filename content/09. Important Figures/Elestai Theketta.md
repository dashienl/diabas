Elestai Theketta is the pseudonym of Elestariathketrea, a polymorphed [[11. Flora & Fauna/Dragons\|Dragon]] "Human" Author, responsible for the creation of world renowned anthology series [[06. Cultural Artifacts/Natura Phenomenicon]]. The series details a complete and total history of the world, including parts which were thought to have been erased during [[The Great Flight of the Heavens]].
