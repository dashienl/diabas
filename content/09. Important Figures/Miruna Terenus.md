# Miruna Terenus, The Vigilant
Miruna Terenus is a [[Tiefling]] Cleric adventurer best known for her time as a member of [[06. Groups/The Righteous 8]]. Her title in the group was The Vigilant, named for her swift thinking that saved the party on numerous occasions. After retiring from adventuring she settled in [[Enyhit]], [[01. Countries/Mitcra]], where she started a family.

### Personal Life

Currently she hasn't taken up a different job, electing to be a mostly stay-at-home single mother for her children. 

She has had 3 children - Crtomir, Rutz, and Tarek - though Crtomir is currently missing as a run-away. She has had 2 partners, both of which are absent or deceased. She had married her second partner by domestic partnership declaration.
